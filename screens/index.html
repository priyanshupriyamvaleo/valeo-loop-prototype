Tier 3 — Onboard, Activate, Renew
These are deliberately lower fidelity — that's a strategy call, not unfinished work. Building all nine at Tier-1 polish would be exactly the failure the plan warns against: thirty equally-polished screens is decoration with a project plan attached.
